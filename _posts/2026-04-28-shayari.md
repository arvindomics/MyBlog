<p style="text-align:left; font-style:italic; font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.8;">
<strong>Mauka Mila Agr kabhi to Chnd Alfaaz Likhunga,</strong><br>
<strong>Kuchh Krne ki Daud me Dam Todti khushiyon ki Aawaz Likhunga,</strong><br>
<strong>Bitaye Lamhon, Guzrare waqut, Dhundti Nazrein me Chhipe Armaan Likhunga,</strong><br>
<strong>Pahonch Sake Tum Tak Jo Bin Kahe Wo Main Paigam Likhung,</strong><br>
</p>
<p style="text-align:left; font-style:italic; font-size: 1.2em; line-height: 1.8;">
<strong>Likhunga Main Wo Har Baat, Har Wo Zazbaat Likhunga,</strong><br>
<strong>Palat Kr Dekh Lena Bhari Nazron Se Jaate Jaate,,</strong><br>
<strong>Phir Milne Ki Aas me, Main Apne Hisse Intezaar Likhunga,</strong><br>
<strong>Anjaan Raaston Pe, Phir Mile Kabhi to Bas Mushkura Dena,</strong><br>
<strong>Khushi Ho Tum Meri, Khuda to Ye Baat Main Har Baar Likhunga, Har Baar Likhunga, Har Baar Likhunga...</strong>
</p>
